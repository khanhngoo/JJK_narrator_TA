# JJK Narrator AI Teacher

## What This Is

A web app where Jujutsu Kaisen fans ask any question and receive explanations in the dramatic JJK narrator style — the slow, detailed, technique-breakdown voice that reveals hidden mechanics behind cursed techniques. Explanations are delivered via cloned JJK narrator voice for full authenticity.

## Core Value

Entertainment-first education that transforms boring concepts into epic JJK-style revelations. Theun isn't just learning — it's experiencing knowledge the way Gojo would explain his Domain Expansion.

## Context

- Target audience: Jujutsu Kaisen fans seeking entertainment with educational value
- Inspiration: The JJK anime's narrator voice that explains Domain Expansions, Cursed Techniques, and sorcery mechanics with dramatic flair
- Style reference: "Who is the one who made Domain Expansion possible?" — that level of dramatic exposition applied to any topic
- Voice cloning is essential for authenticity — fans want the real narrator experience

## Constraints

- **Tech Stack**: React/Next.js for frontend — familiar ecosystem, good for web apps
- **Voice**: Must use voice cloning technology for authentic JJK narrator sound
- **Differentiation**: The JJK humor and community connection is key — not just another AI chat
